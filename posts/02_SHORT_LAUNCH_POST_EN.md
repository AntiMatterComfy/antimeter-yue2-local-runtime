# YuE2 is the open music model I have been waiting for

YuE2 is not just “another text-to-music model.” It writes an editable melody-and-chord plan, then renders a complete 48 kHz song with vocals and arrangement.

The authors' WildSongBench result puts YuE2 best-of-8 above Suno v5 on their SongBench average. That does **not** prove a Suno V6 replacement—but it does make YuE2 the most exciting open music release I have heard in a while.

I made a free ComfyUI starter pack: a local-runtime node for Windows → WSL and native Linux, one-command installers with GPU/VRAM checks, a ready workflow, direct official model links, and prompt examples. The supported tier needs NVIDIA CUDA/BF16 and 24 GB VRAM; 16–23.9 GB cards are clearly marked experimental.

Download the free pack, try the demos, and post your results with the prompt + `cot` mode used.
